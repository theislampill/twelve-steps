# The Twelve Steps and the prescribed answer

*AA, divine prescription and the Way of the Messengers — edition 0.13.0, 2 October 2026.*

[Open the HTML reader](https://theislampill.github.io/twelve-steps/) · [Read the essay](THESIS.md)

AA asks a person to seek God’s help for alcoholism while already offering the
spiritual way through it. Suggestions, individual understanding of God and liberty
of creed make the invitation sound open. The sequence nevertheless supplies the
course through which God’s direction is to be understood.

If God’s prescription is already clear, non-negotiable and sufficient, another
spiritual answer has no place to complete it. The programme’s appeal names God as
the One who directs while putting its own sequence in the place of that direction.
The criticism concerns this displacement within the offer itself.

The [essay](THESIS.md) examines the programme’s grounds and the resemblance between
leaving belief open and leaving the remedy undecided. Its affirmative answer is
the Way of the Messengers, accepting the Qur’an and Muhammad’s teaching as revelation.
On those grounds, AA’s offer has no standing as another spiritual solution beside
the sufficient prescription.
