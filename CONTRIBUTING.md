# Contributing

A useful contribution identifies a sentence, explains the problem and proposes a
replacement. For a factual correction, include the primary source and exact page or
passage. For a logical objection, specify the premise or inference being challenged.

The essay adopts the Way of the Messengers as its affirmative religious contrast;
the companion chapter develops its authority, sufficiency, content and applications.
Preserve that
commitment and distinguish it from findings about AA’s texts and the logical transitions
under examination. Do not derive the religious commitment from a gap in AA.
A quotation establishes what its source says; an interpretation must show how the
conclusion follows. A constructed case must remain labelled as one. Reports about a
fellowship, instructions in a book, and a member’s personal account are different kinds
of evidence. Do not turn one into another by changing its label.

## Method and prose

Write a self-contained argument for a reader familiar with AA. State what the
programme offers, what gives that offer its authority, and what follows when God’s
prescription is already clear, binding and sufficient. The concern is the spiritual
solution itself, including the condition of its appeal to God’s direction. Do not
make the essay a commentary on an earlier submission or an editing instruction.
Do not replace it with a demand for an exclusion rule, a later veto or evidence about
every meeting. Nor should the verdict leave the competing offer standing by changing
its label to assistance. A separate question about attendance must remain separate.

Assess the premises candidly: authority and sufficiency of the given religious answer,
rejection of unauthorised additions, and the religious role performed by AA’s offer.
Sequence alone does not establish that role; quoted accommodation does not ground
the whole solution. The assurance research supplies evidence inside this inquiry.
A correction must address the actual inference rather than replace the question.

The essay applies Ibn al-Qayyim’s analysis of resemblance: persuasive wording can
make a proposition appear sound, while unattractive wording can obscure truth. His
counsel requires examining the meaning impartially.[^shubuhat] In the essay’s
[full application](THESIS.md#how-the-wording-makes-the-answer-look-open), the programme’s
proposition is stated plainly and then its reassuring expressions are restored.
The comparison asks whether changing the presentation changes what supplies the
remedy. The analysis must show what the expressions make the offer appear to leave open.

Keep qualifications that change meaning when removing a formulation’s appeal.
Do not turn a suggestion into compulsion, individual understanding into the equal
truth of every belief, or religious non-affiliation into a denial that God gives
instruction. Conversely, do not accept a reassuring description as deciding what
the offer does. Establish the underlying claim from the sources and assess it
against stated premises. Attractive wording is not proof of falsehood, and an
objection is not false merely because it challenges this essay.

In the supplied *Creed of Asfahani* translation, printed pp. 36–37, Ibn Taymiyyah
reconstructs an argument, gives two responses, and grants a disputed description for
one of them. That provides a model for a bounded reply, not a rigid template for his
whole work.[^method] The related discipline is to distinguish a word’s possible meanings
before judging the claim: *faith*, *help*, *will*, *human power* and *spiritual* need
that care in both AA’s prose and the critic’s.

Grant a claim’s strongest reasonable reading before answering it. Preserve probability
words, conditions, autobiographical scope and the possibility of evidence from more
than one source. Do not treat every fallible judgement as worthless or a possible
alternative as a demonstrated explanation. Apply the same evidential standards to
AA, a Muslim adaptation and the project’s own religious arguments.

Write clear adult English with UK spelling. Prefer a verb and a concrete subject to
a coined cluster of abstract nouns. The essay defines **AA’s case** once; use that
term consistently in the sense defined there. Name a particular passage or inference
when a narrower reference will do. Preserve exact quotations, including their spelling.
Use **Higher Power** consistently
in the project’s own prose.

Each major distinction and objection should have one full treatment. Link back to it
elsewhere. This is an editorial application of the method, not an inherited instruction
from Ibn Taymiyyah about document structure. Keep formal notation in the appendix and
release history in the changelog. Additional words or repeated qualifications should
earn their place by resolving a real ambiguity.

Markdown is the source. Keep the eight-document structure and regenerate the single
offline reader from it. Check section links, footnotes, source fidelity and readable
layout before distributing a revision. A successful mechanical check verifies those
properties; it does not validate a theological premise or recovery claim.

## Submit a correction

For this unpublished archive, return a proposed replacement or unified diff to the
person who supplied it through the same conversation or delivery channel. If a copy
is later hosted in a repository that accepts contributions, a pull request against
that repository can identify the changed passage and supporting evidence. No public
repository address or monitored inbox is implied here.

Submit original prose under CC BY 4.0 and original code under MIT, as specified in
[LICENSE](LICENSE). Mark third-party quotations and identify their sources. Criticise
claims without identifying private participants, publishing meeting disclosures or
speculating about someone’s motives, illness or faith.
